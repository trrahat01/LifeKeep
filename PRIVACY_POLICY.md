# LifeKeep Privacy Policy and Data Safety

**Effective date: October 23, 2026**

LifeKeep is an offline-first personal organizer for notes, tasks, shopping lists, birthdays, important information, and bookmarks. This policy explains what information the app handles, how third-party advertising works, and how you can delete your data.

## Information LifeKeep stores on your device

The information you enter in LifeKeep, including notes, tasks, lists, birthdays, bookmarks, preferences, and app-lock settings, is stored in the app's local storage on your device. LifeKeep does not require an account, and the app does not send this organizer content to a LifeKeep server.

The optional app lock stores a SHA-256 hash of the four-digit PIN you choose. If you enable biometrics, the device operating system performs biometric authentication; LifeKeep does not receive or store your fingerprint or face scan. The app lock is an additional access barrier, not encryption for your organizer data. Someone with access to your unlocked device or app storage may be able to access that data.

## Google Mobile Ads and data safety

The Android app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK and currently uses Google's test ad units. Test ads are for development and testing; they do not generate revenue. The app requests non-personalized ads. A non-personalized ad request does not mean the advertising SDK collects no data.

According to Google's [Google Play Data safety guidance for the Mobile Ads SDK](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure), the SDK may automatically collect and share the following data for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance:

For the Google Play Data safety form, account for these SDK data types as collected and shared. The applicable purposes documented by Google for the SDK include advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

| Data type                              | How it may be used                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Approximate location                   | The device's IP address may be used to estimate general location.                                                  |
| App activity / app interactions        | Interactions such as app launches, taps, and video views.                                                          |
| App info and performance / diagnostics | App and SDK performance information, such as launch time, hang rate, and energy usage.                             |
| Device or other IDs                    | Identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, and other applicable device or account identifiers. |

Google states that data collected by the Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google may process this information under its own terms and [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can review Google's [Privacy & Messaging information](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/10105530) and the advertising ID controls in your Android device settings.

This summary describes the current app integration and the SDK disclosures available when this policy was written. Google's SDK behavior and disclosure requirements can change. Before replacing test ad units with live ad units, LifeKeep's advertising configuration, consent handling, this policy, and the app's Google Play Data safety form must be reviewed and updated to match the actual release.

## Data sharing, security, and accounts

LifeKeep does not upload locally stored organizer content to a LifeKeep server. The app's Google Mobile Ads SDK shares the advertising-related data described above with Google. If you export or share a backup using your device's sharing interface, the apps or services you choose may receive that backup and process it under their own policies.

The app does not offer account creation or login, including login with an account created elsewhere. LifeKeep does not transmit your locally stored organizer content to its own servers. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit; data sent to an app or service you select when sharing an export is governed by that service.

## How to delete your data

To delete organizer content stored by LifeKeep on your device:

1. Open **Settings** in LifeKeep.
2. Under **Danger Zone**, tap **Reset All Data**.
3. Confirm the reset.

This deletes the organizer data stored locally by LifeKeep. You can also clear LifeKeep's app storage or uninstall the app in your device settings. Export or back up anything you want to keep before doing so.

LifeKeep has no account system or LifeKeep server copy of your organizer content, so there is no LifeKeep account or server data to request deletion from. The in-app reset does not delete information processed by Google Mobile Ads or copies you exported to other apps or services. Use Google's privacy controls for information processed by Google, and contact the relevant destination service to manage exported copies.

## Permissions and device features

Biometric authentication is used only if you choose to enable and use that feature. Available methods and permission prompts depend on your device and operating system. You can manage device permissions through your system settings.

## Children's privacy

LifeKeep is a general-purpose organizer and is not designed specifically for children. The app does not require accounts and does not send organizer content to a LifeKeep server. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process the advertising-related data described above. The app's intended audience and ad configuration should be set accurately in the app store and in accordance with applicable policies.
