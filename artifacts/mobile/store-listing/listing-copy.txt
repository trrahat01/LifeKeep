Full description
Keep everyday life organized with LifeKeep, an offline-first personal organizer for the things you want to remember.

NOTES
Write and organize notes. Pin or favorite important notes, use color tags and checklist-style notes, and search when you need something.

TASKS & SHOPPING
Keep to-do tasks with priorities and track completion. Build a shopping list and check items off as you shop.

BIRTHDAYS & SAVED INFORMATION
Save birthdays with countdowns, keep important information in a vault, and organize bookmarks for quick access.

MADE FOR YOUR ROUTINE
• Search across your notes and saved items
• Choose a dark or light theme and accent color
• Optional app lock with PIN or device biometrics
• Export or share a backup from Settings

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE
LifeKeep stores organizer content locally on your device and does not require an account. The optional app lock adds an access barrier; it does not encrypt stored organizer data. Backups you share are handled by the app or service you choose.
